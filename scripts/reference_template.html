Amaravathi, Andhra Pradesh

Published rates.
Not a prediction.

Amaravathi has single-digit live listings and no usable dataset, so HomeCast does not train a model for it — doing so would be fabrication. This page looks up government-published rates instead, shows every source with its own date, and prints the arithmetic in full.

RATES
PRE-2025

Every rate here predates 2025. The Andhra Pradesh Revenue Minister confirmed on 2025-01-29 that the Amaravati capital region — Thullur, Mangalagiri and Tadepalli mandals — was excluded from the February 2025 statewide market-value revision, so official guideline values there were left unrevised. Treat every figure below as an official position of its stated date, not a current market price.

What this cannot tell you

No apartment rate exists

Only plot and land rates could be verified. No official ₹/sq.ft. figure for a flat in the capital region was obtainable, so this page refuses to price one rather than inventing a number.

Not Amravati, Maharashtra

Amaravathi, Andhra Pradesh is a planned capital under construction. Amravati, Maharashtra is a different, unrelated city with a near-identical name and far more listings — mixing the two is the most common way this market gets priced wrongly.

Rates, not transactions

These are administratively fixed prices and auction upset prices, not a record of what buyers actually paid. Real transactions can sit well above or below a notified rate.

How to refresh them

IGRS: registration.ap.gov.in → property value search → Guntur district → Thullur / Mangalagiri / Tadepalli mandal → village. APCRDA notifications and standing orders: crda.ap.gov.in. Add each with its own source and date.
